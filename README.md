# KR Cold War: The Long Armistice

A Hearts of Iron IV submod that brings the Kaiserreich timeline into the Cold War era.

## Overview

**KR Cold War: The Long Armistice** explores an alternate Cold War where the victors of the Weltkrieg face off in a divided world. This preview version (0.1.1) introduces the foundational systems and early content for a Kaiserreich-based Cold War experience.

## Features

- **Bloc Economy System**: Economic management tied to superpower blocs
- **National Development**: Multi-tier development mechanics with GUI integration
- **Proxy Wars**: Dynamic civil war interventions with escalation mechanics
- **Geneva Ceasefire Regime**: Diplomatic framework for managing conflicts
- **Spheres of Influence**: Influence projection, treaty systems, and alignment mechanics
- **Balance of Power**: Dynamic tracking of global power distribution
- **Mission System**: Dynamic objectives for major and minor powers
- **1950 OOB Rebalance**: Historical division counts based on GDP and regional factors

## Requirements

- Hearts of Iron IV version **1.19.***
- **Cold War Iron Curtain: A World Divided** (required dependency)

## Installation

### Steam Workshop (Recommended)
Subscribe to [KR Cold War on Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3810141533)

### Manual Installation
1. Download the latest release
2. Extract to `Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/`
3. Enable both CWIC and KR Cold War in the launcher
